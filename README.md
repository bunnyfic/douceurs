## ✿ &nbsp;what is douceurs?
https://douceurs-jfdy.netlify.app/#main

*Douceurs* is French for "sweetnesses", and that is exactly what this little journal is: a soft, pink corner of the internet devoted to dessert. Part storybook, part play-space, part recipe box, all wrapped up in ribbons.

&nbsp;

## ♡ &nbsp;inside

&nbsp;

**✿ &nbsp;the journal**
*A short, dreamy read about the history of sweetness, four soft things to adore, the small joy of sharing, and a few gentle tips for sweet makers.*

**♡ &nbsp;le petit menu**
*Your own tiny dessert table. Pick pretty sweets, place them on the plate, make them larger or smaller, give them a little tilt, and arrange something that looks just right to you.*

**❀ &nbsp;the recipe box**
*Pink vanilla cookies, strawberry cream cake, soft milk bread rolls, and a cozy buldak carbonara for the nights that want something different. Each one opens like a little card.*

## ❀ &nbsp;the look

Blush pinks and warm cream, scalloped edges, handwritten-style titles, and tiny hearts tucked into every corner. Everything is designed to feel like a pastry box opened on a quiet afternoon.

&nbsp;

## ✦ &nbsp;how to enjoy it

Open **`douceurs.html`** in your browser, and that's all. Turn the page, set your plate, and stay a while.
